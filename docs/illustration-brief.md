# Illustration brief

Use the existing `public/assets/images/tcrs_logo.webp` mascot as the character
reference for future illustrations. Keep the wide golden hat,
rectangular black glasses, gray mustache and pointed beard, friendly
expression, blue work clothes, brown shoes, tool belt, and bold outlines.

Keep the palette warm and limited: cream, blue, tan, brown, and restrained red.
The character should remain recognizable and approachable, with proportions
and expression consistent with the original. Avoid stock-character styling,
trade-specific claims, embedded text, and visual details that imply credentials
or services the owner has not confirmed.

Possible future scenes, after service scope is approved:

- Property upkeep: mascot reviewing a short checklist or carrying a toolbox.
- Remodeling: mascot discussing a measured project plan.
- Heating and cooling, electrical, or plumbing: only create illustrations if
  those services are approved for publication.

The homepage uses `public/assets/illustrations/hero-truck-mascot.webp`, an
illustrated white short-hood utility truck matching the supplied design
reference, with a separate cab, a tall service body with visible side storage
doors, and an overhead ladder rack. Do not replace it with an elongated modern
pickup or invent rear-facing doors. Keep the mascot and the full truck inside
the frame.
Generic project-conversation illustrations are in
`public/assets/illustrations/`; they show discussion and planning only, not
services being performed. The circular mascot badge and badge-and-wordmark
horizontal and stacked logo versions use the original raster art from
`public/assets/brand/`. The badge uses a red field and preserves the entire
hat, glasses, face, and raised hand. Clip the torso at the outer badge rim,
not the smaller character image bounds, and never mask the hat or face.
Full-body logo variations are provided in horizontal
and stacked formats for light and dark backgrounds. The contact banner uses
`public/assets/illustrations/contact-mascot.webp`, a separate thumbs-up pose
matching the original character, with transparent padding around the entire
hat, hands, and tool belt. Keep this pose specific to the contact call to action.

Three older service-concept scene illustrations are for internal layout review
only. They are kept in `app/assets/illustrations/drafts/` and used by the
development-only homepage design preview. Do not publish them unless the
depicted work is confirmed and approved. Do not add project photos or customer
reviews without genuine owner-approved source material.
