# Illustration brief

Use the existing `public/assets/images/tcrs_logo.webp` mascot as the character
reference for any future service illustrations. Keep the wide golden hat,
rectangular black glasses, gray mustache and pointed beard, friendly
expression, blue work clothes, brown shoes, tool belt, and bold outlines.

Keep the palette warm and limited: cream, blue, tan, brown, and restrained red.
The character should remain recognizable and approachable, with proportions
and expression consistent with the original. Avoid stock-character styling,
trade-specific claims, embedded text, and visual details that imply credentials
or services the owner has not confirmed.

Possible future scenes, after service scope is approved:

- Property upkeep: mascot reviewing a short checklist or carrying a toolbox.
- Remodeling: mascot discussing a measured project plan.
- Heating and cooling, electrical, or plumbing: only create illustrations if
  those services are approved for publication.

The current site reuses the original mascot; no new illustrations have been
created.
